# Prompts

Written for pixel art image generators (PixelLab, Retro Diffusion), but they work in any image generator.
Always attach or load `cafe-palette` if the tool allows it, and set isometric / 2:1 when there's an option.

## Style line (paste at the end of every prompt)

> cozy isometric pixel art, 2:1 isometric projection, clean pixel grid, no anti-aliasing, warm wood tones, cream walls, sage green accents, bright warm lighting from the upper left, limited palette, transparent background

## Full scene (use as a mood target, then split into pieces)

> A small cozy corner café seen from an isometric top-down angle, dollhouse cutaway with two tall back walls and two low front walls so you can see inside. Warm wood plank floor, cream walls with wood wainscoting, a big window on the left wall, a wooden counter with a curved glass pastry case full of pastries, an espresso machine and a tip jar, a chalkboard menu on the back wall, a tall wooden bookshelf in the back corner, an upright piano with a small record player on top, a cushioned window seat with pillows, one round café table with two bentwood chairs on a round rug, two hanging plants with trailing vines, fresh flowers in small vases. Outside: a strip of sidewalk and street on the two open sides, a front door with a sage green and cream striped awning, a wooden planter box with flowers, a chalkboard A-frame sign on the sidewalk, and an old street lamp. Minimal, uncluttered, bright and inviting.

Night version: same prompt, then add
> at night, dark blue sky in the window, warm lamp light glowing inside, the street lamp lit, soft pools of light on the floor

Daytime version: add
> in the late morning, bright sunlight coming through the window, soft shadows

## Single objects

Generate these one at a time for editable, separate sprites.

- **Pastry case:** a curved glass bakery display case on a wooden counter base, four pastries on white plates inside: a croissant, macarons, a cinnamon roll, a blueberry muffin
- **Counter:** a long wooden café counter with a sage green tiled front and a wooden top
- **Espresso machine:** a small cream and chrome espresso machine with two cups
- **Tip jar:** a small glass jar with a few gold coins and a paper label
- **Menu board:** a wooden-framed chalkboard menu with hand-lettered text lines
- **Bookshelf:** a tall wooden bookshelf with colorful books, a small plant and a bread basket on top
- **Piano:** a dark wood upright piano with a cushioned bench and sheet music, a small record player on top
- **Window seat:** a built-in wooden window seat with a sage green cushion and two pillows
- **Table set:** a round white marble café table with brass trim and two bentwood chairs on a round rug
- **Hanging plant:** a cream pot on a wall bracket with long trailing green vines
- **Door:** a wooden café front door with a glass window, sage green frame and a small striped awning
- **Lamp post:** a black old-fashioned street lamp with a glowing lantern
- **A-frame sign:** a small wooden chalkboard A-frame sign reading "special of the day"
- **Planter:** a wooden planter box full of pink, yellow and white flowers
- **Avatar:** a small cute pixel character, 16 by 28 pixels, simple face, walking cycle, isometric facing down-right
